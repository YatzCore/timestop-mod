# Ultimate Time Stop (Fabric)

Ultimate Time Stop delivers true engine-level temporal manipulation for Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric Loader. Freeze mobs and hostile projectiles in mid-air, walk across liquid surfaces, punch incoming arrows back at attackers, or dive into movement-driven Superhot combat.

---

## Features

- **Time Stop**: Total world stasis. Freeze all entities, projectiles, falling blocks, and weather. Left-click frozen projectiles to slap/redirect them; right-click to pluck them into your inventory. Walk safely across water and lava.
- **Slow Motion**: Dilation down to fluid slow motion (configurable, default 0.25x / 5 TPS).
- **Matrix**: World simulation slows down, while the wearer retains 100% movement and attack speed.
- **SUPERHOT**: Time progresses only when you physically move or attack. Moving the mouse to aim crosshairs maintains full stasis.
- **Deceleration Field**: A localized shield slowing incoming hostile projectiles by 80%.
- **Fast Forward**: Accelerates world simulation and block entities (furnaces, blast furnaces, smokers, brewing stands) to 5x speed (100 TPS).
- **Speed Calibration GUI**: Press **H** (or click Settings in the watch GUI) to customize Slow-Mo, Matrix, and Superhot speeds to your exact preference.
- **Localized Temporal Bubbles**: Survival watches project localized spherical fields, keeping server tickrates stable at 20 TPS outside the bubble.

---

## Pocket Watches & Runes

Craft 5 survival watch tiers: **Copper**, **Golden**, **Diamond**, **Netherite**, and **Creative**.

Press **Shift + Right-Click** with a watch to open the socket tray and equip up to 14 tactical combat runes:
- **Rune of Redirection**: Parries incoming projectiles back toward attackers.
- **Rune of Snatching**: Intercepts projectiles straight into your inventory.
- **Rune of Phasing**: Auto-teleports away from fatal projectile hits.
- **Rune of Kinetic Amplification**: Multiplies melee strikes during stasis with 2.5x launch force.
- **Rune of the Tachyon**: 3x faster mining and instant attack cooldown recovery in slow motion.
- Plus **Orbital Redirection**, **Spatial Transposition**, **Kinetic Barrier**, and more.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Right-Click** | Activate or stop selected temporal mode (with Pocket Watch in hand) |
| **Shift + Right-Click** | Open Watch Management GUI and Rune Socket Tray |
| **V** | Cycle active time mode |
| **H** | Open Temporal Engine Configuration & Speed Calibration GUI |
| **K** | Toggle projectile flow (suspended vs flowing stasis) |
| **R** | Launch orbiting projectile shield (Orbital Rune) |
| **G** | Swap positions with targeted entity (Transposition Rune) |
| **Middle-Click** (Hold) | Deploy Kinetic Barrier (Neo's Palm) |

## Requirements & Installation

- **Minecraft**: 1.21.1
- **Mod Loader**: Fabric Loader 0.16.10 or higher
- **Dependencies**: Fabric API 0.116.17+1.21.1 or higher
- **Java**: 21

1. Ensure Fabric Loader and Fabric API are installed.
2. Place `timestop-fabric-1.21.1-1.4.0.jar` into your `.minecraft/mods` directory.
